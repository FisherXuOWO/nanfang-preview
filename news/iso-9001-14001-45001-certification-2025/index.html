Wuxi South earns ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification in a single audit

WUXI, China, 18 July 2025 — Wuxi South Petroleum Additives Co., Ltd. has been certified to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 by China Quality Mark Certification Group (CQM), a member of the IQNET international certification network. The three certificates were issued together on 18 July 2025 following a combined audit of the Wuxi site and are valid until July 2028.

What is covered

The certified scope is the production of detergents, dispersing agents and antioxidant/anti-corrosion agents, together with the related management activities, at No. 48 Daqiao Road, Shitangwan, Huishan District, Wuxi. That covers every single-component product line on this site: sulfonate, phenate and salicylate detergents, ashless dispersants, ZDDP and ashless antioxidants.

  • ISO 9001:2015 — quality management: how raw materials are qualified, how each batch is produced, tested and released, and how customer complaints and changes are handled.
  • ISO 14001:2015 — environmental management: control of emissions, wastewater and waste at the plant, which runs its own wastewater treatment facility and VOC abatement equipment on emission points.
  • ISO 45001:2018 — occupational health and safety: the systems behind a plant that has operated for decades without a safety accident.

A system with a long history

Wuxi South first obtained ISO 9002 certification in 1998 and upgraded to ISO 9001:2000 in 2001; the quality system has run continuously since. The 2025 audit extends that system to environmental and occupational health and safety management under the current editions of all three standards, with a single certification body and a single surveillance cycle.

Why it matters to formulators

For a customer, a certificate is only as useful as the practices behind it. In day-to-day terms it means that the properties printed on each technical data sheet — TBN, elemental content, viscosity, flash point — are tested to the GB/T, SH/T or ASTM method named on the sheet, and that the certificate of analysis shipped with each lot reports what our laboratory actually measured. Buyers who audit suppliers can request the certificates and the underlying procedures from their sales contact; copies of all three certificates are also published on the About Us page.

About Wuxi South Petroleum Additives

Wuxi South Petroleum Additives Co., Ltd. has manufactured lubricant additives in Wuxi, Jiangsu, since 1976. The company produces single-component additives — calcium and magnesium sulfonate, sulfurized calcium phenate and sulfur-free calcium salicylate detergents, ashless succinimide dispersants, ZDDP antioxidant and anti-corrosion agents and ashless antioxidants — for lubricant formulators, blenders and grease makers in more than 50 countries. Long-standing customers include Sinopec and PetroChina. Technical data sheets, treat-rate guidance and samples are available from the product pages on this site.
