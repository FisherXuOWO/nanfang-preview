About Wuxi South

Lubricant Additive Manufacturer
Since 1976.

Wuxi South Petroleum Additive Co., Ltd. has produced lubricant additives since 1976. From our site in Huishan District, Wuxi — on the Grand Canal, 130 km from Shanghai and 60 km from Jiangyin Port — we manufacture a broad portfolio of metal detergents (phenates, salicylates, sulfonates), ashless dispersants and ZDDP anti-wear chemistries used in automotive and marine engine oils around the world.

Our senior management are recognised industry experts, each with decades of experience in additive technology, manufacturing and sales. Around 45% of output is exported to more than 50 countries; at home we are a key supplier to Sinopec, PetroChina and China Railway, and our products are also used in military vehicles and ships.

Technology & R&D

Component Chemistry, Proven on the Bench

Our technology base comes from the Research Institute of Petroleum Processing (RIPP) and the Shanghai Refinery Research Institute, developed further through joint programmes with international additive companies — including the sulfur-free salicylate series co-developed with Chemtura.

Detergent Chemistry

Sulfonate, phenate and salicylate detergents from low-base to overbased (TBN 20–400), including sulfur-free salicylates co-developed with Chemtura.

Thermal-Process Dispersants

Mono, bis, borated and high-molecular-weight succinimides made by chlorine-free thermal adduction on BASF polyisobutylene.
